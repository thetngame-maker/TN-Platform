# TN Game OS 5.131.0

## Private Adventure Planning Notes

- Adds a compact, collapsed notes panel to active Saved Adventures.
- Saves up to 600 characters of private planning context per Explorer and plan.
- Supports explicit updates and clearing without reloading the library.
- Rejects edits to archived plans and clears notes from duplicated plans.
- Keeps notes out of sharing, calendar exports, maps, printable itineraries, and public pages.
- Adds no background save, bulk action, external connection, or notification service.
