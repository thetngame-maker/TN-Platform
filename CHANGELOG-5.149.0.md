# TN Game OS 5.149.0

## Adventure Prep Organizer

- Adds a combined 0–10 launch score to every scheduled, non-archived Saved Adventure card.
- Adds Needs Prep and Launch Ready filters for upcoming private plans.
- Updates card status, progress, and the active preparation filter after explicit checklist changes.
- Excludes unscheduled, past, archived, and unrelated plans from both preparation filters.
- Derives the organizer from existing private rendered data and stores only the existing device-local view preference.
