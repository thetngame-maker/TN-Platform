# TN Game OS 5.105.0

## Community Photos + Activity Feed

- Adds a signed-in community photo uploader tied to Universal Map places.
- Sends checkpoint and community uploads through a dedicated moderation queue.
- Awards 25 Explorer XP exactly once when an administrator approves a photo.
- Adds Community Photos to TN Game OS administration with pending, approved, and rejected views.
- Builds the public Explorer feed from approved photos and the canonical gameplay event ledger.
- Adds All, Photos, Discoveries, and Milestones feed filters.
- Preserves upload attribution, captions, place relationships, and checkpoint context.
- Adds a compact, touch-friendly activity and upload experience for approximately 390px screens.
- Keeps unapproved photos out of the public feed.
