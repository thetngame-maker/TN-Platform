# TN Game OS 5.130.0

## Adventure Readiness Checklist

- Adds an owner-only four-item readiness checklist to scheduled Saved Adventures.
- Covers current conditions, reservations or tickets, directions, and gear.
- Updates the card and Next Adventure readiness totals immediately after each confirmed change.
- Resets the checklist when the adventure date changes and omits it from duplicated plans.
- Rejects archived, unscheduled, or unsupported readiness updates on the server.
- Adds no reminder service, bulk action, public exposure, or background write.
